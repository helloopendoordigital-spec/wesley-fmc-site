About Wesley Church

Wesley is a Christ-centered church for all people, regardless of religious background. Through uplifting worship, Biblical preaching, personalized prayer, and a number of other ministries, we seek to help individuals of all ages be renewed by the power of God.

"Church isn\u0027t where we meet. Church isn\u0027t a building. Church is what we do. Church is who we are. Church is the human outworking of the person of Jesus Christ."

Our History

Wesley Free Methodist Church had its beginning with a small group of people who were hungering and thirsting after scriptural Christianity.

The first meeting was in a local Holiday Inn in October 1969. Services began the next Sunday in a Seventh Day Adventist Church on West 8th Street in Anderson. The group of believers grew so rapidly that they needed to move to the Northside Junior High School to accommodate their growth and they remained there until they were able to move into their new facilities at 3017 West 8th Street.

The congregation was known as Wesley Chapel and was affiliated with another denomination until June 1983. On January 1, 1984 the congregation officially became a part of the Free Methodist Church USA and was named the Wesley Free Methodist Church.

The name "Wesley" identifies the church and its members with the great scriptural doctrines proclaimed by John Wesley and the Methodist movement.

Over the years, the church has grown, purchased additional property and added a Christian Education Wing and a Family Life & Community Center which includes a full gymnasium.

Senior Pastors who have served Wesley include: Max Morgan, Keith Eckhardt, Jeff Blake, Elmer Riggs, John Clark, Harvey Fry, John Lane, Mark Murphy, Marc Adams, Blake Chenoweth, and presently Mike Hopper.

Our Beliefs

+

At Wesley Free Methodist Church, we believe that the Body of Christ includes all who trust and follow Jesus. We also believe that the Bible is God's written Word, uniquely inspired by the Holy Spirit and completely trustworthy in all it affirms.

God is the Creator of the heavens and the earth and is sovereign, perfect and holy.

Jesus Christ is the divine Son of God who came to offer Himself on the Cross for the forgiveness of our sins and to provide salvation to all who respond in faith. Salvation, which is solely by personal faith in Jesus Christ, manifests itself in the life of the believer in the sincere desire to live life according to God's will, primarily as revealed in the Bible.

The experience of the Spirit-filled life is God's promise to every follower of Jesus and occurs as one is given totally to God and results in a life of holy living and loving witness for Christ.

The Lord Jesus Christ will return to earth someday. Those who died in the faith will be resurrected, and as followers of Christ will be taken to heaven, along with all believers then living. All who have rejected the good news of life in Jesus will spend eternity separated from God.

Our Vision & Mission

+

Wesley Free Methodist's Mission: "To make and multiply growing disciples for Christ."

This mission, developed by the Wesley Church Board of Ministry, is based on the Great Commission (Matthew 28:19-20) and helps guide the ministry we do as we seek to make disciples who make disciples.

Just what does a "disciple" look like? The Bible provides many insights into this. A disciple is a born-again follower of Jesus Christ who…

  • Studies and lives according to and shares God's word.
  • Grows spiritually through prayer and worship (private & corporate).
  • Lives a Spirit-filled life with compassion and integrity.
  • Exemplifies servanthood through the fruit of the Spirit.
  • Participates as a team player using spiritual giftedness.
  • Makes other disciples.

Please join us as we allow God to work through us as together we seek to make disciples who make disciples.

Ministries & Programs

Jesus told us to make disciples of all nations. Here are the ways we serve.

Music & Worship

God-honoring worship is something we strive for. Many church members get involved in our music and worship ministry. Some play their instruments. Some are vocalists. These volunteers are an important part of the ministry of Wesley Church, using their talents and gifts to glorify God.

Children's Ministries

Our goal is to raise kids to become fully devoted followers of Jesus Christ. During Sunday worship, children ages 4-10 are invited to Children's Church — a special time with songs, games, prayer, and Bible study using videos and visual aids. We also hold special monthly children's events.

Adult Ministries

Sunday School at 9:30 AM (Koinonia, Discovery & Bereans classes), Women's Bible Study (Tuesdays 9:30 AM), Monday Evening Bible Study (2nd & 4th Mondays at 5:30 PM). Groups designed to connect people in a loving, caring atmosphere.

Guest Services

The Guest Services teams fill an important role — directing attendees to their seats, ensuring building safety, guiding traffic, helping visitors, answering questions, and ensuring services proceed smoothly. A servant-hearted group integral to church life.

Media & Technology

Using media to spread the gospel is a privilege. In an age of widespread technology, we use these tools to honor God. Our weekly sermons are available on our Facebook page. We maintain an active social media presence for church communication.

Prayer Warriors

Prayer cannot be overestimated. Prayer moves the hand of God. We are a body of believers dependent upon prayer. We pray for our pastors, church members, special requests, and missionary needs. We have an active prayer chain — contact Kathy Walker at (765) 617-6412.

Sports Ministry

Our gymnasium hosts Unguardable Basketball (running nearly every day), Community Pickleball (Sundays 6-8 PM at courts or gym), Men's Basketball (Tuesdays 7:30 PM), and Pastor Al Davis Basketball. Open to the community!

Homeschool Co-op

Wesley hosts the White River Christian Home Educators (WRC). The homeschool co-op meets Mondays from 6:30 AM to 4:30 PM, plus Religious Education on Fridays. A vital community resource for homeschooling families.

Community Garden

Garden plots are available to reserve for free. Enjoy gardening with others in your community and add to the beauty of Wesley with your own creativity. Open to all!

Community Pickleball

Each Sunday evening (6-8 PM), individuals are invited to come learn how to play and join friendly competition. Depending on the weather, pickleball is played either at the courts on 10th St. and Madison, or in the Wesley Church gymnasium. For more info, contact coordinators Dennis and Diana Delph.

Journey with Jesus

+

Life can be described in terms of being on a spiritual journey. God, the great creator and designer of life, provides guidance in His Word, the Bible. The central theme of the Bible is that God loves us and sent his son, Jesus, that we might have eternal and abundant life.

God's Love: "For God so loved the world that He gave His one and only Son, that whoever believes in Him shall not perish but have eternal life." — John 3:16

We Are Sinful: "For all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God." — Romans 3:23

Sin Has a Penalty: "For the wages of sin is death." — Romans 6:23

Christ Paid Our Penalty: "But God demonstrates His own love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us." — Romans 5:8

Salvation is a Free Gift: "For it is by grace you have been saved, through faith — and this is not from yourselves, it is the gift of God — not by works, so that no one can boast." — Ephesians 2:8-9

Christ is at Your Heart's Door: "Here I am! I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears My voice and opens the door, I will come in." — Revelation 3:20

The Christian life is a journey with God. The Wesley Free Methodist Church is here to assist you along that journey. Let's walk together and find strength from God and encouragement from one another!

Narcotics Anonymous

NA is a group of recovering drug addicts who meet regularly to help each other stay clean. There is only one requirement for membership — the desire to stop using. There are no initiation fees or dues, no pledges to sign, no promises to make. Anyone may join, regardless of age, race, sexual identity, creed, religion or lack of religion.
